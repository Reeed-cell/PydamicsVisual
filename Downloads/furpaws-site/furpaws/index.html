Good to know

Questions people usually ask.

Do I need to book ahead, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means less waiting — especially for surgery or diagnostics, which need a bit of prep. If it's urgent, come straight in; we'll fit you in.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any past medical records or vaccination history you have, and your pet on a leash or in a carrier — even calm pets can spook in a new place.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash on-site. Call ahead if you need to check on other options for your visit.

Do you treat animals other than cats and dogs?

Give us a call before heading over — it depends on the case, and we'd rather confirm than have you make the trip for nothing.

What if it's an emergency outside clinic hours?

Call us first at 8425-5965 — if we can't take it, we'll try to point you somewhere that can.
